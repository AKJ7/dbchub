# Notice & Takedown Policy

dbchub hosts community-contributed files. If you believe a file infringes your rights
(copyright, trade secret, etc.), discloses confidential information, or otherwise
violates [`ACCEPTABLE_USE.md`](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md), we will review and act on a report
promptly.

> Replace the contact details below with a real address before going live, and — if you
> operate in the US and want DMCA §512 safe-harbor protection — register a
> **designated agent** with the U.S. Copyright Office.

## How to report

Email **akj123429@gmail.com** (or open a GitHub
issue if the matter is not sensitive) with:

1. Your name and contact information, and your relationship to the rights holder.
2. Identification of the specific file(s) — the path(s) under `databases/` or a link.
3. Identification of the right you hold or the policy violated, and why the content
   infringes it or is not permitted.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the
   rights holder, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you
   are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## What we do

- We review reports and, where warranted, remove or disable access to the file(s),
  typically by reverting the commit and purging the content.
- Because the repository is public and version-controlled, removed content may persist
  in git history and forks until history is rewritten; we will take reasonable steps to
  purge it from this repository.
- We may notify the contributor and record the action.

## Counter-notice

If your contribution was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification,
you may send a counter-notice to the same address with the file identification, your
contact details, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief
the removal was in error, and your signature.
